# Code Of Conduct

Openza Reader should be a practical and welcoming project for people who care about Windows software, Markdown, and careful engineering.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, especially across different levels of experience.
- Keep criticism focused on code, behavior, and project goals.
- Help keep issues and pull requests actionable.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, or sustained off-topic disruption.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the security/contact path listed in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other reasonable moderation steps to protect the project.
